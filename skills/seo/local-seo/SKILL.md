# Local SEO

## Purpose
To optimize a local business's online presence to attract more foot traffic and localized searches (e.g., "plumber near me" or "coffee shop in Brooklyn").

## What This Skill Does
Focuses heavily on the Google Map Pack (the top 3 local results) rather than standard organic listings. It drives immediate, high-intent local phone calls and visits.

## Core Concepts
- **Google Business Profile (GBP)**: The master listing for local SEO. If this isn't optimized, local SEO fails.
- **NAP Consistency**: Name, Address, Phone number. These must be 100% identical across the entire internet.
- **Local Pack / Map Pack**: The 3 local business results shown above traditional organic links.
- **Citations**: Mentions of the business NAP on other websites (Yelp, YellowPages, local directories).
- **Proximity**: How physically close the searcher is to the business. (The #1 ranking factor, which you cannot control).

## Step-by-Step Workflow
1. **GBP Optimization**:
   - Claim and verify the Google Business Profile.
   - Ensure the primary category is hyper-specific (e.g., "Divorce Lawyer" not just "Lawyer").
   - Add high-quality photos (exterior, interior, team).
   - Write a keyword-rich business description.
2. **Citation Building**: Use tools like Whitespark or BrightLocal to build consistent NAP citations across the top 50 local directories.
3. **Review Generation**: Implement a system to ask happy customers for Google reviews. Reply to *every* review, incorporating local keywords in your replies where natural.
4. **On-Page Local SEO**:
   - Ensure the NAP is in the website footer.
   - Embed a Google Map of the location on the Contact page.
   - Use `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD schema markup.
