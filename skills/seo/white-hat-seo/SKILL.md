# White-Hat SEO

## Purpose
To execute Search Engine Optimization entirely within the bounds of Google's Webmaster Guidelines, ensuring zero risk of algorithmic or manual penalties.

## What This Skill Does
Focuses on long-term, sustainable growth. It prioritizes the user experience, high-quality content creation, and earning natural backlinks rather than manipulating search algorithms.

## Core Concepts
- **Compliance**: Strict adherence to Google Search Essentials (formerly Webmaster Guidelines).
- **User-Centric**: Writing content for humans first, search engines second.
- **Earned Media**: Getting backlinks because the content is genuinely good, not because a link was paid for or exchanged.
- **Longevity**: White-hat tactics take longer to see results (3-6 months) but build a highly defensible moat that survives core algorithm updates.

## Step-by-Step Workflow
1. **Technical Foundation**: Ensure the site is fast (Core Web Vitals), mobile-friendly, secure (HTTPS), and easily crawlable without deceptive cloaking or hidden text.
2. **Keyword & Topic Research**: Identify questions the target audience is asking.
3. **Content Creation**: Publish 10x content (content that is 10 times better than the current #1 result). Cite sources, use original data, and provide expert analysis (E-E-A-T).
4. **Outreach**: Conduct digital PR or genuine outreach to promote the content to journalists and bloggers, asking them to read and share it (without offering compensation).

## AI Agent Instructions
IF auditing a strategy for white-hat compliance:
1. VERIFY that all backlinks were acquired without financial compensation or reciprocal exchanges.
2. VERIFY that content is not programmatically generated at scale without human review (SpamBrain compliance).
3. VERIFY that the content shown to Googlebot is identical to the content shown to the user (No cloaking).
